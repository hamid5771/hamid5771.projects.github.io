Featured independent project · Applied NLP

Hospitality Review Intelligence

Separate the hotel stay from the dining experience. An interactive review-analysis prototype that uses DeBERTa-v3 to analyse Google Travel and TripAdvisor feedback about hotel services and restaurant experiences.

For managers

Compare hotel and dining sentiment, explore monthly trends and review summaries, and investigate recurring service themes. Filter by date, platform and rating, with optional duplicate exclusion and credibility weighting.

For guests

Adjust the balance between room comfort, sleep and check-in versus restaurant and breakfast experiences to explore a personalised, review-based match score. Browse supporting reviews by traveller type.

  • Python
  • DeBERTa-v3 · ABSA
  • Transformers
  • Streamlit
  • Plotly
  • SQLite
  • Playwright
Try the interactive demo View source on GitHub

The demo uses a fixed dataset with precomputed sentiment scores. Match scores reflect review sentiment and selected preferences; credibility weights are heuristic. Service themes are grouped by keywords. An independent project, not commissioned or endorsed by Premier Inn.
